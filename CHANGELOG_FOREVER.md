## v0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

First Artisan Nexus build for WoW Forever.

### Added

- Artisan Nexus now runs on WoW Forever, as its own download next to the retail version.
- Session Loot, the Artisan Hub, crafting profit, the craft queue, the shopping list and the Auction House tools work as they do on retail.
- Systems that WoW Forever does not have stay out of sight: concentration, profession knowledge, crafting orders, gathering overload and the Warband bank.
- Every character keeps its own data, even when two characters on a realm share a first name.
